The initial net-income provisions distinguish A (motorized vehicles with four or
more wheels), B (motorized two-wheel vehicles), and C (nonmotorized transport
or no vehicle). The original Fifth provision lists 36%/30%/12%, but the Second
Transitory instead sets 60%/50%/15% for the first three months from effectiveness
(July–September). The October and January amendments then change the applicable
schedule. Interpret each month using its applicable transitional provision or
amendment. See [S03](p17-source-register.md#s03-initial-net-income-rules)
and amendments S04–S05. These rules do not authorize estimating net platform
income from ENOE's general monthly labor-income field.

## Population and administrative timing

LFT Article 291-C in [S01](p17-source-register.md#s01-lft-decree) distinguishes
people meeting a monthly net-income threshold of at least one monthly Mexico
City minimum wage from those below it, with specific occupational-risk
protection. This substantive rule does not define the coding of a CSV column.

IMSS [S02](p17-source-register.md#s02-imss-rules), 4.1(b), requires initial
registration from platform registration; 4.1(d–f) then determines eligibility
at month end and submits removals and salary adjustments in the first five
calendar days of the following month. Section 4.2 also addresses occupational
injury below the threshold. Consequently initial registrations, people meeting
the monthly threshold, affiliated jobs and unique persons are separate targets.
Do not label them interchangeably or infer the publication's revision/cutoff
policy from the legal deadlines alone. Section 4.1(a) permits a national
employer registration or registrations by IMSS administrative unit: this is
additional reason to validate geography before comparing residence domains.
